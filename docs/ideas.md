# Hackathon Ideas

Here are 10 possible hackathon ideas to brainstorm from. I kept them broad so we can narrow them down once you share the hackathon theme, track, or judging criteria.

## 1. AI Study Buddy
A study assistant that turns notes, PDFs, and lecture slides into quizzes, summaries, and flashcards.

**Why it works:** Easy to demo, useful for students, and naturally shows off AI.

## 2. Smart Expense Splitter
An app that tracks group spending, splits bills fairly, and reminds people who owes what.

**Why it works:** Simple core workflow, practical, and good for a polished MVP.

## 3. Campus Event Finder
A discovery app for campus events with filters for clubs, interests, date, and location.

**Why it works:** Clear UI opportunity and easy to populate with mock or real events.

## 4. Mental Wellness Check-In
A lightweight app that helps users track mood, habits, and daily check-ins with gentle recommendations.

**Why it works:** Strong human value and room for thoughtful design.

## 5. Local Volunteer Match
A platform that matches volunteers with nearby community service opportunities based on skills and time.

**Why it works:** Social impact angle with straightforward matching logic.

## 6. Interview Practice Coach
An app that gives mock interview questions, feedback, and role-specific preparation tips.

**Why it works:** Great for AI assistance and easy to explain to judges.

## 7. Smart Meal Planner
A meal-planning tool that suggests recipes based on budget, dietary needs, and ingredients already at home.

**Why it works:** Very demo-friendly and useful for everyday life.

## 8. Personal Productivity Dashboard
A dashboard that combines tasks, calendar, goals, and habits into one focused view.

**Why it works:** Good if you want a clean frontend and solid UX.

## 9. Emergency Helper App
An app that quickly shares location, emergency contacts, and first-aid steps during urgent situations.

**Why it works:** High-impact idea with a clear problem statement.

## 10. Hackathon Team Matcher
A platform that helps participants find teammates based on skills, interests, and project ideas.

**Why it works:** Meta-hackathon idea, easy to understand, and useful immediately.

## Quick Recommendation
If you want the strongest hackathon MVP candidates, I’d shortlist:
- AI Study Buddy
- Hackathon Team Matcher
- Smart Expense Splitter
